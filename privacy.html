The three things that matter most.

1. Nothing leaves your phone unless you sign in. Zen Lock's free features need no account — your meditation history and lock settings stay on the device.
2. We can never see which apps you chose to lock, and that data is technically incapable of leaving your device.
3. We do not sell data, we run no ads, and the app contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Who provides this app

Zen Lock (晨鎖) is built and operated by Chon Kit Lam, an individual developer based in Macao, who is also the data controller for the purposes of this policy.

For any privacy question, request, or complaint, write to zenlock9512@gmail.com and I will answer personally.

Without an account, nothing leaves the device

Zen Lock's free features require no account at all. Until you choose to sign in, all of the following stays on your phone and is never sent to any server:

  • Your name, avatar initial, and your answers during onboarding
  • Every meditation record (time, duration, mood) and your streak
  • All preferences: lock time, volumes, language, notification toggles
  • The apps you chose to lock

Signing in is required only to subscribe, so that your subscription follows you to a new phone or a different platform.

What we collect once you sign in

Account data

You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive a user identifier and your email address, used to identify your account, sync your data back to your devices, and contact you when necessary.

With Sign in with Apple you may choose Hide My Email — in that case we only ever receive a relay address supplied by Apple, never your real inbox.

Synced progress and settings

After you sign in, the following is transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored in our database (Supabase, hosted in Singapore), for one purpose: so you get your progress back after changing phones or reinstalling.

CategoryContents
Meditation progress Streak, best streak, total minutes, and a record of each completed session (end time, actual duration, the mood you selected before and after)
Profile The name you entered, avatar initial, goals and onboarding answers
Preferences Language, lock time and schedule type, the four volume levels, notification toggles

This is an allow-list: only the fields listed above are ever synced.

Subscriptions and purchases

Payment is handled by Apple. Subscription status is validated and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat (United States), which processes purchase identifiers, transaction records, subscription state, and device-level technical information such as country and the iOS tracking-consent status.

We never see your payment method or card details. Those stay with Apple.

What never leaves your device

  • Which apps you chose to lock. That selection is an opaque token issued by Apple's Screen Time, meaningful only on your device. We cannot read it, and we deliberately never sync it — which is why you must re-select your apps after switching phones. That is the cost of the design, and we think it is worth paying.
  • Your notification history, whether today is unlocked, and on-screen draft selections.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.
  • We serve no advertising and use no data for ads or cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking. The app ships with no analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDK of any kind.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos. The app requests none of these permissions (microphone access is explicitly disabled at build time).

If we later show an anonymous figure such as "how many people completed their ritual this morning", the request will contain a date and nothing else — no identifier, no random UUID, no account information. That feature is currently switched off.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists — that is what it is for.

You can delete your account from inside the app: Settings → Delete account. Your row in our database is then immediately and permanently deleted, including every meditation record and setting. Deleting the account and deleting the data are the same single operation here; there is no half-deleted state.

Deleting the app removes all local data from the device.

Two things outside our control: purchase records Apple retains for tax and refund obligations, and any subscription period you have already paid for — cancel a subscription in iOS under Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Zen Lock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you are a parent and find that your child has created an account without your consent, write to us and we will delete it.
