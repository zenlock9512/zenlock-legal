常見問題

為什麼要給「螢幕使用時間」的權限?

晨鎖可以鎖住你想要鎖的 App。而 iOS 上唯一能做到這件事的機制,就是 Apple 的「螢幕使用時間」(Family Controls)。

這個權限不會讓我們看到你用了什麼 App、用了多久。你選的那份清單是 Apple 給的一個不透明代號,我們解讀不了它 —— 連我們自己也不知道你鎖了哪幾個。

換了新手機，為什麼要重新選一次要鎖的 App?

因為那份選擇綁在那台裝置的「螢幕使用時間」上下文,是一個只在該裝置上有意義的不透明代號。搬到另一台不會解析成同樣的 App。

這不是我們偷懶沒做同步 —— 是 Apple 的設計就不允許。其他資訊(連續天數、每一次冥想的紀錄)只要你登入過就會回來,只有這一項要重選。

忘了做儀式,手機一直鎖著怎麼辦?

鎖定畫面上有緊急解鎖。它會直接解開今天的鎖,不需要冥想。

代價是連續天數會歸零 —— 那是刻意的,否則它就變成一條比冥想更方便的捷徑。

冥想做到一半離開會怎樣?

什麼都不會發生。不解鎖、不扣連續天數、也不會把今天算成已完成。

中途離開等於沒發生過,重來一次就好 —— 我們不想讓「放棄」變成一件有懲罰的事。

一定要註冊帳號嗎?

不用。免費功能完全不需要帳號,而且在你登入之前,所有資料都留在你的手機上,不會傳到任何地方。

只有訂閱需要登入。那是為了讓訂閱跟著你的帳號走 —— 換手機都拿得回來。

怎麼取消訂閱?

訂閱由 Apple 管理,不在晨鎖裡面。到 iPhone 的「設定」→ 最上方你的名字 → 「訂閱項目」→ 晨鎖 → 取消訂閱。

晨鎖的設定頁也有一個「管理訂閱」會直接帶你去那一頁。

⚠️ 在當期結束前至少 24 小時取消,否則會被收下一期的費用 —— 免費試用期也一樣。

怎麼刪除帳號?

晨鎖的「設定」→「刪除帳號」。按下去之後,我們資料庫裡屬於你的那一列會立即且永久地刪除,包含所有冥想紀錄。

用 Apple 帳號登入的話會再跳一次 Face ID —— 那是為了向 Apple 撤銷這個 App 的授權,讓它從你「使用 Apple 帳號登入」的清單裡消失。

冥想時沒有聲音?

先檢查 iPhone 側邊的靜音開關,以及音量鍵。

如果只有某一層沒聲音(例如有頌缽但沒有背景音),打開冥想畫面的音量轉盤——那裡有四軌獨立的音量:頌缽、背景音、氛圍音、呼吸語音。其中一軌可能被調到了最小。

Common questions

Why does Zen Lock need Screen Time permission?

Zen Lock blocks the apps you choose to block, and Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) is the only mechanism on iOS that can do that.

This permission does not let us see which apps you use or for how long. Your selection reaches us as an opaque token issued by Apple, which we cannot read — we do not know which apps you locked.

Why do I have to pick my apps again on a new phone?

Because that selection is bound to that device’s Screen Time context: an opaque token that only means something on the device that created it. Move it to another phone and it does not resolve to the same apps.

This is not a sync we skipped — Apple’s design does not allow it. Everything else (your streak, every meditation record) comes back as soon as you sign in. Only this one has to be re-selected.

I forgot to do the ritual and my apps are still locked.

The lock screen offers an emergency unlock. It opens today’s lock immediately, without meditating.

The cost is that your streak resets — deliberately, otherwise it would be an easier route than the ritual itself.

What happens if I leave a meditation halfway?

Nothing at all. Your apps do not unlock, your streak is not deducted, and the day is not marked complete.

Leaving halfway is as though it never happened. Start again whenever you like — we did not want giving up to carry a penalty.

Do I need an account?

No. The free features need no account, and until you sign in, everything stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Only subscribing requires signing in. That is what lets a subscription follow your account — change phones and you get it back.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside Zen Lock. Open Settings on your iPhone → your name at the top → Subscriptions → Zen Lock → Cancel Subscription.

Zen Lock’s own Settings screen has a "Manage subscription" row that takes you straight there.

⚠️ Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, or you will be charged for the next one — this applies to the free trial too.

How do I delete my account?

Zen Lock’s Settings → Delete account. Your row in our database is then immediately and permanently deleted, including every meditation record.

If you signed in with Apple, Face ID appears once more — that is us revoking this app’s authorisation with Apple, so it disappears from your "Sign in with Apple" list.

There is no sound during meditation.

First check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone, and the volume buttons.

If only one layer is missing (the bowl sounds but the background does not, say), open the volume dial on the meditation screen. It carries four independent levels: bowl, background, ambience, and breathing voice. One of them may be turned all the way down.
